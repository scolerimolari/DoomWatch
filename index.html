07 // PERSONAL PREPAREDNESS

Preparedness is about practical resilience, not panic. Prioritise realistic local risks and official safety advice.

FIRST 24 HOURS

  • Check official local alerts and avoid spreading unverified claims.
  • Charge phones and power banks; conserve battery.
  • Check water, essential medication and immediate household needs.

24–72 HOURS

  • Use a modest rotating supply of food and drinking water.
  • Plan for heating, cooking and lighting safely if utilities fail.
  • Agree check-ins, meeting points and support for vulnerable neighbours.

LOCAL CONTINUITY

  • Know alternative routes if roads, ports or public transport are disrupted.
  • Keep key phone numbers and documents available offline.
  • Follow PSNI, NIFRS, NI Direct, Met Office and utility instructions.

PRACTICAL DISRUPTION SCENARIOS

01 // POWER OR WATER INTERRUPTION
  • Check NIE Networks or NI Water service notices and estimated restoration information.
  • Keep fridge and freezer doors closed; use chilled food safely and discard anything unsafe.
  • Use torches rather than candles where possible. Never run generators, barbecues or outdoor heaters indoors.
  • Store a sensible amount of drinking water and prioritise vulnerable household members.
02 // FUEL, FOOD OR TRANSPORT DISRUPTION
  • Check whether disruption is confirmed and local, or only a national/international headline.
  • Prioritise essential journeys, check alternative routes and allow extra time.
  • Rotate ordinary pantry supplies rather than panic-buying; keep essential dietary and pet needs covered.
  • For heating oil, check supplier lead times early and compare dated local price information.
03 // CYBER OR TELECOMS DISRUPTION
  • Keep key phone numbers, meeting points and essential documents available offline.
  • Use mobile data or another trusted connection only where safe; verify service notices through official channels.
  • Do not open unexpected links or attachments. Change compromised passwords from a trusted device and enable MFA.
  • Do not forward screenshots or claims as fact until the original source and date are checked.
