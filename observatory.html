RELIABILITY & EVIDENCE STANDARD
GRADE A — OFFICIAL / PRIMARY

Government, regulator, operator or primary catalogue. Strong provenance, but may be delayed, incomplete or narrowly scoped.

GRADE B — ESTABLISHED / INVESTIGATIVE

Established newsroom, independent investigation or research organisation. Check documents, named sources and corrections.

GRADE C — SPECIALIST / ADVOCACY

Specialist publisher, advocacy organisation or aggregator. Useful leads; check methodology and underlying evidence.

GRADE D — COMMENTARY / ALTERNATIVE

Commentary and alternative publishers. Monitor narratives and leads; independently verify factual claims.

GRADE E — SOCIAL / COMMUNITY

Unverified user-generated content. Treat as a lead only; identity, location, timing and context may be wrong.

GRADE U — UNCLASSIFIED

Insufficient publisher metadata to assign a source class. Do not infer reliability from appearance or repetition.

Evidence labels are inherited from upstream records or describe a specific catalogue record. Claim confidence remains unassessed unless a numeric value was supplied upstream. Reliability grade is never converted into a confidence score.

IMPORTANT LIMITS: OpenSky now requires OAuth2 for automated API use; Reddit requires approved API access; Telegram/X are not treated as unrestricted data sources; AIS services may be commercial; Cloudflare Radar API access requires a token; RIPE Atlas needs selected measurements. These connectors are deliberately not bypassed. Copernicus records are catalogue metadata only, not interpreted satellite imagery. Economic Stress is currently a qualitative signal stream, not a validated composite index.
