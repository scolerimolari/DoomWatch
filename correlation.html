06 // METHOD, RELIABILITY & LIMITATIONS

HOW THE ENGINE WORKS

Records are drawn from the 90-day intelligence archive and its six observatory views, then deduplicated by canonical URL or a conservative title/source key. Topic matching is transparent keyword classification. Clusters require distinct publisher domains; source count is not equivalent to independent corroboration.

SOURCE GRADES

A = official / primary; B = established or investigative; C = specialist / advocacy / aggregator; D = commentary / alternative; E = social / community; U = unclassified. Grade describes source class, not claim truth or confidence.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No automated attribution, sentiment-to-threat conversion, predictive probability, risk score or declaration of an incident. It does not access private groups, bypass APIs or verify images, identities, locations or claims. Repeated syndicated stories and several outlets citing one original source can look like multiple publishers.

ANALYST CHECKLIST

Open the original sources. Compare publication times and named locations. Identify whether reports share a press release, wire service, agency or social post. Look for primary confirmation. Separate direct observation from interpretation. Record disconfirming evidence before escalating a hypothesis.
