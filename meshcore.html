MeshCore & the M9

A reference workspace for understanding the MeshCore network, finding public UK and Ireland resources, and preparing an M9 companion radio. This page is informational: it does not collect field observations, maintain node registers, store message archives or record checklists.

Connectivity limitation: DoomWatch is not connected to your M9 or any radio. It cannot receive private messages, inspect local radio traffic, verify coverage or provide live telemetry. Public directories and community resources are not proof that a node or room is currently active.

01 / MeshCore project

MeshCore is an open-source, decentralised LoRa mesh communications system. Network participation depends on compatible hardware, firmware, regional radio rules, channel configuration and nearby nodes.

02 / M9 deployment sequence

  1. Confirm the exact M9 model and firmware version.
  2. Charge the unit and check the manufacturer’s setup guidance.
  3. Install the official compatible companion application.
  4. Check Bluetooth discovery and pairing on Android.
  5. Verify region, frequency and channel settings against current local regulations.
  6. Test a basic message with a known contact before relying on it.

Browser BLE access depends on Android browser support, secure-context permissions and firmware capabilities. Direct DoomWatch control is not currently available.

04 / Radio and legal considerations

  • Use only frequencies, power levels and antenna configurations permitted in your jurisdiction.
  • Do not assume a public room is monitored or suitable for emergency communication.
  • Mesh messages may be delayed, duplicated, incomplete or unavailable when nodes are offline.
  • Do not transmit personal, sensitive or operationally critical information over an open channel.
  • Maintain an independent fallback communication method.
