HOW TO READ THIS VIEW

Location is derived from source metadata and text matching. Some reports are national or global and cannot be reliably placed on a local map.

Counts describe records in the current collector snapshot, not the number of confirmed incidents, affected people or active outages.

For high-impact claims, open the original publisher and compare independent reporting. Repeated syndicated copy is not independent corroboration.
